🏆 MANAGER TOURNAMENT 🏆

The knockout bracket gives eight managers a balanced path from quarter-finals through the semi-finals to a central grand final.

How it works and rules:

  1. Entry fee is 100 KSh (fully optional to join).
  2. The tournament structure displays side-by-side draws just like the UEFA Champions League tournament tree, tracking matches from quarter-finals up to a final, with an admirable trophy graphic featured right in the center.
  3. The knockout tree is built for eight managers: four quarter-final matches feed two semi-finals and one central final.
  4. The Admin manually registers the participants, sets the matchups, and updates the draws. The tournament standings table will automatically arrange and update itself in a sensible manner.
  5. The ultimate winner who takes home the total cash pot is the manager who accumulates the most total points over the specified number of consecutive game weeks set for the tournament!

The KSh 100 registration fee is paid once for the entire tournament, not per gameweek. The prize estimate multiplies the number of confirmed entrants by KSh 100; Admin confirms payments and the final payout.

1 · Register

Entry is a one-time KSh 100 per manager for the whole tournament. It is not charged again for each gameweek. Admin writes the confirmed participants and tracks the collected entry pot.

2 · Play the groups

The tournament runs over the gameweeks Admin sets. Admin enters each manager’s group record and combined FPL points for those gameweeks. Group tables sort by group points, goal difference, goals scored, then name.

3 · Draw and advance

Admin enters the participants and draws fair random pairings. After recording each round’s winners, Admin can draw the next round automatically through the final.

4 · Prize winner

The manager with the highest combined FPL points across the tournament gameweeks wins the KSh 100-per-entry pot and is shown with the cup. A points tie is decided by group points, goal difference, goals scored, then manager name. The knockout bracket tracks progression; it does not decide the cash prize.

🔄 BENCH SWAPPING 🔄

Bench swaps are managed using the published participation rules and current gameweek status.

If you want to use it, the process and strict requirements are:

  1. Send 30 shillings to the Admin.
  2. The Admin will swap a player from your starting 11 with the player on your bench who has the highest points.
  3. Strict Starting 11 Requirements: The player being taken out of your starting 11 must match at least ONE of these conditions:
    • They did not start or play the game at all.
    • They received a red card.
    • They were injured during the match.
  4. Special Captain Rule: If you want to swap out your Captain, he must have exactly 0 points for the swap to be valid.

How Bench Swapping works

  1. Send your manager name, the starting player, and the reason for the request.
  2. The request fee is KSh 30; Admin confirms payment before approving a swap.
  3. The replacement must be the highest-scoring eligible player on your substitutes bench.
  4. The starting player must be injured or have a red card; zero points qualifies only when that starting player is your captain.
  5. Admin checks the player status, points, captain status, and payment before confirming the outcome.
Late contribution penalty: Pay by the countdown deadline. A KSh 30 penalty applies to a contribution recorded after it. Admin checks payment time and marks the payment status.
